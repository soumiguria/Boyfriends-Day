My Love,

If you were here right now, I probably wouldn't know what to say properly. I'd probably just look at you, smile like an idiot and hug you.

So instead, I made this little place for you. A tiny piece of my world that you can visit whenever you want.

I know distance isn't always easy. There are days when I wish I could simply reach over, hold your hand, annoy you in person and steal your food.

But somehow, even from far away, you have become such a huge part of my everyday life.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for staying. Thank you for all the little things you probably don't even realize I notice.

And if you ever wonder whether you're loved... please come back here.
